# Phase 5: Repurposing & Multi-Channel Distribution Blueprint

## 1. Phase Overview & Strategic Objective

### Purpose
Phase 5 transforms a single piece of core content (blog post, newsletter, podcast/video transcript, case study, or past high-performing post) into a **complete multi-channel publishing engine**, extracting 10x ROI without sounding like repetitive copy-paste spam.

### Core Objectives
- **Pillar-to-Micro Derivation**: Extracts 4–8 platform-native derivative assets from 1 long-form source.
- **Native Formatting Calibration**: Adapts each derivative to the exact cultural and algorithmic physics of LinkedIn, X, Instagram, and short-form video.
- **Time-Staggered Distribution**: Schedules derivatives with a **48–72 hour stagger window** across channels to prevent follower fatigue.

## 4. Time-Staggered Multi-Channel Scheduling Protocol

To prevent audience fatigue among users who follow the brand on multiple platforms:

```
Day 1 (Mon): Publish Hero Asset on LinkedIn (e.g. 7-slide PDF Carousel)
   └── (48-hour gap)
Day 3 (Wed): Publish Thread Spoke on X (Twitter) (Punchy 5-tweet thread)
   └── (24-hour gap)
Day 4 (Thu): Publish Reel Video Script on Instagram / TikTok / Facebook
   └── (48-hour gap)
Day 6 (Sat): Publish Standalone Quote / Takeaway Tweet on X
```
