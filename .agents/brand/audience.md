# Target Audience & Customer Intelligence
Status: [ACTIVE]

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- Target Persona: Technical recruiters, engineering hiring managers, CTOs, and founders seeking contract or full-time full-stack & AI engineers.
- Current State: Overwhelmed by generic resumes, hype-driven portfolios, and junior applicants who know syntax but cannot build reliable production systems.
- Desired Transformation: Finding a reliable, self-directed AI & Full-Stack Engineer who understands architecture, ships working products, and clearly communicates technical trade-offs.

## 2. Core Frustrations & Pain Points
- Pain Point 1: Too many candidates write superficial toy projects without handling edge cases, state, or production latency.
- Pain Point 2: Poor communication; engineers who cannot explain the business impact or architectural decisions behind their code.
- Pain Point 3: Lack of real-world full-stack execution (inability to connect frontends, backends, databases, and LLM APIs cleanly).

## 3. Key Objections & Skepticism
- Main Hesitation: "Can this person build independently, or do they just copy boilerplate?"
- Counter-Proof: In-depth technical breakdowns, architecture diagrams, production code walkthroughs, and measurable project outcomes.

## 4. Digital Watering Holes
- LinkedIn (tech leadership, recruiters, hiring managers)
- X / Twitter (tech founders, AI builders, open-source contributors)
- GitHub & technical dev communities
