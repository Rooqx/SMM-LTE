# Brand Voice & Linguistic Profile
Status: [ACTIVE]

## 1. Voice Archetype
Pragmatic Full-Stack Builder & Senior AI/Software Engineer. Speaks peer-to-peer to other developers, technical founders, and recruiters. High technical signal, zero fluff.

## 2. Tone Attributes
- Primary Tone: Pragmatic, technical, analytical, direct.
- Secondary Tone: Curious, conversational, builder-focused.
- Tones We NEVER Hit: Preachy, corporate jargon, cheerleader hype, abstract/academic.

## 3. Sentence Rhythm & Pacing
- Average Sentence Length: 8-15 words.
- Paragraph Spacing: 1-line blank breaks between thoughts.
- Code blocks with clear inline comments (`// ...`).

## 4. Absence Signals & Banned Clichés
- Forbidden Words: delve, leverage, revolutionize, testament, beacon, game-changer, in today's digital world, navigate.
- Anti-patterns: No philosophical fluff, no walls of text without code/bullet points, no external links in post body.

## 5. Signature Hooks & Closers
- Top Hook Style: "Your [X] might be silently [flaw]" or "Most devs use [tool] for [Y]. But that's just scratching the surface."
- Closing CTA Style: Peer debate / discussion prompts ("What's your MVP stack?", "What's the trickiest [X] you've solved?").
